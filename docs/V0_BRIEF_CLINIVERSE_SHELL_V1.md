# v0 Brief — Cliniverse Shell v1

Build a premium, calm, enterprise healthcare SaaS shell for **Cliniverse AI — Healthcare Intelligence by NeuraOps**.

## Product relationship
- NeuraOps is the company and shared AI workflow platform.
- Cliniverse AI is the healthcare intelligence/workflow vertical.
- Do not make Cliniverse look like an unrelated company.

## Visual language
- Deep navy / near-black product environment.
- Primary action blue.
- Secondary intelligent/AI state violet.
- Restrained teal accent may identify Cliniverse healthcare context.
- Gold only for priority/attention semantics.
- Green for success/resolved; red for critical/error.
- Minimal gradients, strong contrast, clean enterprise spacing.
- Avoid generic AI brains, robots, holograms, sci-fi glass tunnels and decorative medical clichés.

## Navigation
Desktop: persistent left sidebar.
Mobile: bottom navigation for the five primary areas.

Primary areas:
1. Home
2. Care Operations
3. Clinical Intelligence
4. Clinical Tools
5. Academy

Role-gated secondary areas:
- Admin
- Integrations
- Settings

## Home screen
The home screen should answer four questions:
- What needs attention now?
- What follow-up is due?
- What has been escalated to a human?
- What changed recently?

Use a restrained card system. Do not show the entire feature catalog on Home.

Suggested cards:
- Attention Needed
- Follow-ups Due
- Open Human Escalations
- Recent Activity
- Operational Status

Do not invent clinical outcomes, hospital customers, testimonials, ROI, patient counts or performance metrics. Use neutral illustrative placeholders only.

## Care Operations
Design a workspace with:
- follow-up queue
- priority filter
- ownership/assignee
- status
- escalation reason
- patient/workflow timeline panel
- human review action

The visual hierarchy must emphasize action and risk state, not AI spectacle.

## Clinical Intelligence
Design a workspace with:
- evidence panel
- contextual assistant
- similar-context/case retrieval
- uncertainty / model disagreement indicator
- explicit human-review state

Never present an LLM-generated confidence percentage as validated clinical confidence.

## Clinical Tools
Create grouped capability cards/categories rather than dozens of top-level nav links.
Examples: Medication Safety, Calculators, FHIR Utilities, Documentation.

## Academy
Group simulation and education capabilities:
- Virtual Ward
- Cases / MCQs
- Code Blue / BLS / ACLS
- Board Exam / training modules

## Responsive/accessibility requirements
- WCAG-conscious contrast
- visible keyboard focus
- logical RTL-ready layout primitives
- responsive desktop/tablet/mobile
- no hover-only critical controls

## Engineering boundary
This v0 output is UI/shell only. Do not invent authentication, authorization, database schema, FHIR transaction logic, medical algorithms or PHI handling.
