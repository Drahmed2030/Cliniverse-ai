# Cliniverse Brand System v1

Status: **FROZEN FOR RELEASE DIRECTION**

## Brand hierarchy
- **NeuraOps** = company and shared AI workflow infrastructure.
- **Cliniverse AI** = healthcare intelligence and workflow product by NeuraOps.
- **LeadOps** = revenue workflow product by NeuraOps.
- **Afia** = patient-facing Cliniverse experience, separately gated.

## Selected marks
- **NeuraOps:** Edge N — corporate parent mark.
- **Cliniverse AI:** geometric C with central intelligence/spark signal — product mark.

The geometric C is selected over the pulse-line alternative because it:
1. belongs more naturally to the angular Edge N family;
2. is distinctive at app-icon and favicon sizes;
3. avoids implying medical-device measurement, ECG accuracy, monitoring, or diagnosis;
4. can extend to clinic, hospital and enterprise surfaces without looking consumer-only;
5. leaves the pulse/clinical motif available as a secondary illustration, not the legal/product mark.

## Relationship rule
Preferred lockup:

**Cliniverse AI**
**A NeuraOps product**

Apple-facing description may use:

> Cliniverse AI is a healthcare intelligence and workflow application by NeuraOps.

## Shared palette
Corporate family:
- Deep Navy `#0B0F19`
- Navy `#111827`
- Neura Blue `#2563EB`
- Electric Blue `#3B82F6`
- Violet `#7C3AED`
- Light Gray `#E5E7EB`
- White `#FFFFFF`

Cliniverse healthcare accent:
- Clinical Teal `#06B6D4`
- Existing operational teal `#0D9488` may remain for status/interaction semantics during the release refactor.

Gold is not a primary corporate brand color. Keep it only for restrained semantic use such as premium/attention states when justified.

## Typography
Primary UI stack remains system-first for release reliability:
`-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "SF Pro Display", Inter, sans-serif`

Do not introduce a remote font dependency solely for branding before RC1.

## Product design principles
- one visual family, distinct product identities;
- dark/navy foundation with restrained blue/violet/teal accents;
- clinical surfaces prioritize clarity and trust over decorative effects;
- status colors are semantic, not branding decoration;
- no fake live indicators, medical-device styling, or unvalidated clinical certainty;
- Apple AppIcon, favicon, PWA icon and product header must derive from the same approved Cliniverse mark.

## Technical rule
Brand identity is part of release integrity. Final assets must be deterministic in the generated native archive. No placeholder Capacitor icon, conflicting legacy mark, or alternate logo family may ship in RC1.

## Asset authority
The generated concept board is a direction reference only. The final production vector/source asset must be frozen separately before native RC1 packaging. Image assets should not be inferred or regenerated by build scripts from an unapproved source.
