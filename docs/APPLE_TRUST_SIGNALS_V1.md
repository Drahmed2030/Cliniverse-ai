# Apple Trust Signals v1

## Purpose
Strengthen reviewer and user confidence through truthful, inspectable product behavior. This is not a marketing-claims document.

## Release trust signals
- Stable cold launch on supported iPhone and iPad devices.
- Consistent Cliniverse AI product identity under NeuraOps.
- Functional Privacy, Terms and Support routes.
- Reviewer/demo access to the complete submitted release surface.
- Human-in-the-loop positioning for healthcare workflow support.
- No real patient-identifiable data accepted in Apple v1.
- Clinical Intelligence / third-party AI transmission gated until consent and safety controls pass.
- No client-side entitlement activation.
- Clear distinction between educational/workflow features and future institution/enterprise capabilities.
- Accurate App Privacy and accessibility declarations based on final binary evidence.

## Things we deliberately do not use as trust signals
- fabricated customer counts;
- unsupported accuracy percentages;
- fake live patient data;
- implied regulatory clearance;
- unsupported HIPAA/GDPR/PDPL certification claims;
- medical-device imagery or measurements unless actually validated;
- fake hospital partnerships or testimonials.

## Reviewer-facing outcome
A reviewer should see a focused, complete, stable healthcare workflow product with explicit safety boundaries — not a beta platform, hidden feature catalog or medical-device claim.
