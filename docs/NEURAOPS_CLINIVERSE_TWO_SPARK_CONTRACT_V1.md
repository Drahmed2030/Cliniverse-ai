## Brand and product architecture

- **NeuraOps** is the operating company and enterprise intelligence layer.
- **Cliniverse AI** is the first healthcare product created by NeuraOps.
- **Clinical Pathway Replay** and the **Medical Operations Registry** are the first two product sparks inside Cliniverse AI.
- Future NeuraOps products may reuse the governed-agent and operational-intelligence architecture without diluting Cliniverse AI's medical identity.

## The two sparks

### 1. Experience spark — Clinical Pathway Replay

Demonstrates an immediate, legible transformation:

`events → integrity → KPI → gap → targeted training → human closure`

The initial STEMI replay uses fictional simulation data and makes no diagnosis, treatment, activation, validation, or patient-outcome claim.

### 2. Trust spark — Medical Operations Registry

Turns preserved guidelines and standards into governed source records containing:

- publisher and primary URL;
- version or publication state;
- regional applicability;
- intended operational use;
- linked future pathway;
- explicit human-review boundary.

The registry is not a free-form medical encyclopedia and is not an executable clinical decision engine.

## NeuraOps company surface

The future company route is `/neuraops`. It is designed as a responsive Next.js and React progressive-web surface with:

- NeuraOps as the primary company identity and Cliniverse AI as its healthcare product;
- one working-product CTA;

- media-ready product language without fabricated testimonials or adoption metrics;
- evidence and regulatory traceability; and
- accessible keyboard, focus, reduced-motion, and touch behavior.

The existing `/` Apple release entry remains unchanged.

## The leading indicator

The strongest early indicator is **Qualified Replay Activation (QRA)**:

> the percentage of qualified hospital or clinical partners who, after seeing the landing surface, open the replay, reach the gap explanation, and request or accept a pathway-specific discovery session.

The product should not be judged by generic page views. The first decision gates are:

1. At least 5 qualified institutional walkthroughs.
2. At least 3 complete the replay narrative without explanation.
3. At least 2 provide a real pathway problem and agree to a structured discovery follow-up.
4. At least 1 signs a bounded pilot letter or paid discovery agreement.

These are hypotheses and commercial gates, not forecasts or guarantees.

## Evidence architecture direction

The model aligns with:

- WHO SMART Guidelines / Digital Adaptation Kits for structured digitization of guidance;
- HL7 FHIR R5 as a future exchange vocabulary, without a present conformance claim;
- region-specific clinical guidance such as the 2026 Saudi Heart Association focused CCS update;
- European Health Data Space readiness as a future governance and interoperability target.

No guideline becomes an executable rule without scoped clinical review, localization, version control, source licensing review, test cases, and approval ownership.
