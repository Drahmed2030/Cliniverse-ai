# Global Trust Operating Model v1

Status: STRATEGIC OPERATING MODEL — no hiring or spending authorized by this document.

## Company structure
- NeuraOps = company and shared AI workflow infrastructure.
- Cliniverse AI = healthcare intelligence/workflow product.
- LeadOps = revenue workflow product.
- Afia = patient-facing Cliniverse experience, separately gated.

## Trust moat
The company will compete on evidence-backed reliability, transparent AI boundaries, privacy-by-design, human accountability, operational auditability, and consistent product identity — not on unsupported medical claims or feature volume.

## Critical fractional co-worker roles
1. iOS release engineer — Xcode archives, signing, TestFlight, device QA, privacy manifests, App Store delivery.
2. Security/privacy engineer — RLS, auth isolation, secrets, threat modeling, incident readiness and vendor review.
3. Clinical safety advisor — reviews medical wording, evidence boundaries, human-escalation design and high-risk feature gates.
4. Product/UX accessibility reviewer — core-task usability, VoiceOver/contrast/text-size/reduced-motion review and App Store accessibility evidence.
5. Enterprise partnerships lead — hospital/clinic discovery, procurement mapping, pilots and institutional contracts after release.
6. Fractional finance/legal support — entity/contracts, tax/accounting, privacy/commercial terms and recurring-revenue controls.

Founder/CEO remains final product and commercial authority; no role should duplicate ownership without a measurable gate or outcome.

## Technical support stack
- GitHub: source control, review gates, CI evidence and release history.
- Vercel: web/runtime delivery and preview validation.
- Supabase: authenticated data platform with RLS and server-controlled entitlement boundaries.
- Apple Developer / App Store Connect: native distribution, TestFlight, App Review, IAP and product-page trust signals.
- Monitoring/incident tooling: add only when a concrete production reliability requirement exists; avoid tool sprawl.

## Commercial model after Apple v1
- Individual clinician digital features: Apple-compliant purchase path where required.
- Clinic/practice: contracted workflow package with scoped seats/workspaces and server-side entitlement.
- Hospital/institution: enterprise agreement, security review, implementation scope, support SLA and controlled integrations.
- Enterprise/API: only after reliability, privacy and audit controls are validated.

One entitlement authority should map all channels into the same server-side access model. A checkout, invoice or contract by itself never grants client-side privilege.

## Financial leverage to evaluate
- App Store Small Business Program eligibility before enabling paid iOS distribution.
- Cloud/startup credits only when they replace expected spend and do not create harmful lock-in.
- Vendor free tiers/trials before new subscriptions.
- Fractional specialists and scoped audits before full-time hiring until recurring revenue justifies headcount.

No purchase, hire, contract or financial commitment is authorized by this document.

## Global credibility milestones
1. App Store release with truthful metadata and stable native experience.
2. Public Trust/Privacy/Support surfaces with verified company contact routes.
3. First paid customer evidence without fabricated ROI or testimonials.
4. Repeatable pilot delivery and measurable operational outcomes.
5. Independent security/privacy review before handling regulated patient-identifiable data at scale.
6. Institution-ready security, procurement and support package.
7. Documented case studies with customer permission.

## Brand rule
One company, distinct products, one trust system.

NeuraOps should become known for reliable AI workflow infrastructure; Cliniverse should become known for healthcare workflow intelligence that preserves human clinical accountability.
