Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: JULY 28, 2026

Welcome to Cliniverse AI — the clinical training platform built by a physician, for physicians. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app and services at cliniverseai.com.

📋 1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Name and email address when you register
  • Professional details (specialty, clinical rank)
  • Authentication data via Supabase

Usage Data

  • Cases completed, XP earned, accuracy scores
  • MCQ answers and performance metrics
  • App features accessed and time spent

Device Information

  • Device type, operating system, browser
  • IP address and approximate location (country)
  • Device ID for session management

⚡ 2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and improve clinical training features
  • Track your progress, XP, and clinical rank
  • Generate AI-powered cases and MCQ via Claude AI (Anthropic)
  • Process subscription payments via Lemon Squeezy
  • Send important app updates and notifications
  • Maintain the global leaderboard
  • Ensure app security and prevent fraud

🔒 We never sell your personal data to third parties. Your clinical performance data is yours alone.

🤖 3. AI & Third-Party Services

Cliniverse AI uses the following trusted services:

  • Anthropic Claude AI — Powers AI case generation and MCQ. Queries are processed securely. No personal identifiers are sent to Claude.
  • Supabase — Secure database for user accounts and progress. Data stored in encrypted servers.
  • Lemon Squeezy — Payment processing for PRO subscriptions. We do not store card details.
  • Vercel — App hosting and deployment. GDPR compliant.

🔐 4. Data Security

  • All data transmitted via HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Passwords are never stored — authentication via secure tokens
  • Database access restricted with Row Level Security (RLS)
  • Regular security audits and updates

✅ 5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correct — Update inaccurate information
  • Delete — Request deletion of your account and data
  • Export — Download your progress and performance data
  • Opt-out — Unsubscribe from marketing emails anytime

To exercise any of these rights, email us at toffe24@icloud.com and we will respond within 30 days.

💳 6. Subscriptions & Payments

  • PRO subscriptions are processed by Lemon Squeezy
  • We do not store payment card details on our servers
  • You can cancel your subscription anytime from your account
  • Refund requests handled within 7 days of purchase

👶 7. Children's Privacy

Cliniverse AI is designed for medical professionals and students aged 18+. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18 years of age. If you believe a minor has registered, please contact us immediately.

🍪 8. Cookies & Tracking

  • We use essential cookies for authentication and session management
  • No advertising or tracking cookies are used
  • Local storage is used to save your app preferences

🔄 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or an in-app notification. Continued use of Cliniverse AI after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
