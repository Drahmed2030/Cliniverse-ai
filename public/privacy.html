Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

⚕️ Medical Disclaimer: Cliniverse AI is an educational platform designed for healthcare professionals. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the guidance of a qualified physician for medical decisions.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use Cliniverse AI:

  • Account information: Name, email address, medical specialty, training level, and country
  • Usage data: Cases completed, MCQ answers, XP points, streak data, and tool usage patterns
  • Device information: Device type, operating system, and app version
  • Voice data: When using Ambient AI Scribe, audio is processed in real-time and not stored on our servers

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Personalise your clinical training experience
  • Track your progress and award XP and badges
  • Generate AI-powered clinical cases and recommendations tailored to your specialty
  • Send educational notifications and weekly clinical pearls
  • Improve our AI models and platform features
  • Process subscription payments securely

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL) with row-level security enabled. All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256 encryption.

We are hosted on Vercel infrastructure with servers in the United States and European Union.

4. Ambient AI Scribe

🎙️ The Ambient Scribe feature uses your device's microphone to transcribe consultations. Audio is processed in real-time using the Web Speech API (on-device) and sent to our AI service only as text. No audio recordings are stored.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Anthropic Claude API: Powers AI case generation and SOAP notes — text only, no personal data shared
  • OpenAI: Text-to-speech for Arabic/English lectures
  • Supabase: Secure database hosting
  • Lemon Squeezy: Payment processing — we never store card details
  • Vercel: Application hosting

6. FHIR Integration

When using the FHIR Integration feature, you connect to external EHR systems using your own credentials. We do not store or access patient health records. The FHIR sandbox uses publicly available test data only.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

8. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct your information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Export: Download your data in a portable format
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time

9. Children's Privacy

Cliniverse AI is intended for healthcare professionals and medical students aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18.

10. Saudi Arabia & GDPR Compliance

We comply with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where applicable. Users in the European Economic Area have additional rights under GDPR.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via email or in-app notification.
